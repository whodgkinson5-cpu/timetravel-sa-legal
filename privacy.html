Privacy Policy

TimeTravel SA

Location and why we use it

When Location Services is enabled in TimeTravel SA and you use Map Explorer or Journey Mode, the app uses high-accuracy location to show your position and find nearby historical sites. During an active Journey, the app may request background location permission to detect nearby sites and play narration while the app is in the background or the screen is locked. Background location use depends on your device permissions and operating-system behaviour; updates may be paused or unavailable, and stop when you end the Journey or turn Location Services off in the app. You can deny or change location access in your device Settings; map positioning and nearby discovery will then be limited.

What happens to location data

TimeTravel SA uses location coordinates on your device to compare your position with the site catalogue. The app does not upload your GPS coordinates to a TimeTravel SA server or keep a location history. It stores unlocked site identifiers and background discovery cooldown records (site identifiers and timestamps) locally on your device. Local notifications about nearby sites are scheduled on your device.

Maps, network and audio

Map Explorer displays map tiles from the device's native map provider. The provider may receive requests for the map area displayed, which can reveal an approximate area; review that provider's privacy terms for details. The app also makes a basic connectivity check to Google when Map Explorer opens or returns to the foreground. No GPS coordinates are included in that check. Narration is played locally from audio bundled with the app or using your device's text-to-speech. If a story is configured with a remote audio URL, playback requests go to that audio host. Subscription purchases and restores are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

Your choices and deletion

You can stop a Journey at any time with End Journey, deny background access, or change permissions in your device Settings. TimeTravel SA has no account and does not provide cloud location sync. Locally stored app data is generally removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to your device's backup and restore behaviour. Sites marked as heard are kept in memory only for the current app session.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact the app publisher at EarthConnected@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. Effective date: 2 November 2026.
